# Demo Script

## Demo title

Fabric IQ for Telecom Service Assurance and Customer Impact

## Opening

"This demo shows how a telecom provider can use Microsoft Fabric to connect network telemetry, service support, field operations, and business impact through a governed ontology. Instead of analyzing disconnected reports, users can ask business questions and trace issues from customer complaints to network root cause and operational response."

## Scene 1: Executive impact

Show Power BI executive dashboard:

- Complaint rate is rising in Ontario.
- 5G Home Internet has the largest degradation.
- SLA exposure is concentrated in premium and enterprise accounts.
- Churn risk has increased for repeat-impact customers.

Sample question:

> What is driving the increase in customer complaints in Ontario?

Expected answer:

Fabric IQ identifies a cluster of support cases linked to 5G Home Internet service degradation in Toronto West, correlated with packet loss and latency anomalies on affected cell sectors and a congested backhaul link.

## Scene 2: Network operations investigation

Show network operations view:

- Affected regions.
- Cell sectors with degraded network health score.
- Telemetry trends before, during, and after incident.
- Active alarms and incident timeline.

Sample question:

> Which network assets are most likely causing the degradation?

Expected answer:

The ontology traces impacted service instances to specific cell sectors and a shared backhaul link with abnormal utilization, packet loss, and latency.

## Scene 3: Customer and support impact

Show service support view:

- Ticket spike by channel.
- Sentiment trend.
- Repeat callers.
- Case reasons.
- Known-incident linkage.

Sample question:

> Which customers are affected and how should support respond?

Expected answer:

Affected customers are grouped by impacted service instance, customer tier, open case status, and churn risk. Support agents can reference the known incident and expected resolution path.

## Scene 4: Field operations prioritization

Show work order and dispatch view:

- Open work orders.
- Technician availability.
- Priority score.
- SLA exposure by incident.

Sample question:

> Which work orders should be prioritized today?

Expected answer:

Work orders linked to high-severity incidents, premium customers, and high SLA exposure rank highest. The top work order resolves the congested backhaul path affecting the largest number of customers.

## Scene 5: Resolution and outcome

Show post-resolution telemetry:

- Packet loss returns to normal.
- Latency drops.
- Ticket volume declines.
- Incident status changes to resolved.
- SLA exposure stabilizes.

Closing message:

"Fabric IQ turns fragmented telecom operational data into a connected business model. Network operations, support, field teams, and executives can reason over the same governed data foundation."
